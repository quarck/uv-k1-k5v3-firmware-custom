Bonus: going back to stock, offline

Quansheng's official .bin files are XOR-packed with a version field at 0x2000 and a CRC16 tail — the bootloader wants raw. Decode them first:

python3 cli.py decode stock_firmware.bin        # -> stock_firmware.raw.bin
python3 cli.py flash -p /dev/ttyACM0 stock_firmware.raw.bin
